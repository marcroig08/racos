01 · Our place

Six reasons to hit the road.

Some restaurants you go to because they're close by. Others you keep coming back to. Since 1979, the Racó has been the second kind, for Almenara locals and for everyone who drives in from further afield.

Book a table

47years cooking
rice in Almenara

  1. 01 / 06

    Rice since 1979

    Cooking rice in Almenara since 1979. Craft like this can't be improvised: it's practised, paella after paella, until getting it just right becomes second nature.

    Craft

  2. 02 / 06

    Produce you can taste

    Fresh produce, real saffron and Molino Roca rice. No shortcuts: what goes into the pan is exactly what reaches your plate.

    Ingredients

  3. 03 / 06

    Sea and land on one menu

    From arròs negre to lobster rice with king prawns, by way of duck fideuà (our noodle paella) with wild mushrooms and foie. And if today isn't a rice day, there's the grill: Iberian pork abanico or entrecôte.

    Menu

  4. 04 / 06

    Like coming home

    A family restaurant of the old school. Attentive service, generous portions and the kind of atmosphere where lunch drifts into a long, lazy afternoon before you know it.

    Family

  5. 05 / 06

    The Valencian esmorzar

    From 9:00 to 12:00, the mid-morning feast locals swear by: a crusty sandwich on artisan bread, grilled meats, olives, cacaus (roasted peanuts) and allioli. And coffee, of course.

    9 to 12

  6. 06 / 06

    Closer than you think

    Just a quarter of an hour from Sagunt, La Vall d'Uixó or Moncofa. And there's a terrace for sunny days, which around here means nearly all of them.

    Terrace
